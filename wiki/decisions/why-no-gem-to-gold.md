# Why No Gem-to-Gold Conversion

## Decision

There is no way to convert gems directly into gold.

## Rationale

Gem → gold conversion would make the game pay-to-win. A whale could buy unlimited gems, convert to gold, max out equipment upgrades, and dominate PvP with no skill investment.

## Tradeoffs

**Pros:**
- F2P integrity — gold is earned, not bought
- PvP leaderboard reflects skill + time, not wallet
- Gold economy stays healthy (no infinite injection from IAP)

**Cons:**
- Whales have fewer ways to spend — some may churn
- New players can't "catch up" with money

## How Gems Generate Value Instead

Gems buy convenience, not power:
- Stamina refills (play more, but still have to win)
- Protection scrolls (reduce upgrade RNG, not bypass it)
- Battle Pass premium (cosmetic + modest rewards)
- Inventory slots
