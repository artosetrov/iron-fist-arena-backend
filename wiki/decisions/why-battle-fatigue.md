# Why Battle Fatigue

## Decision

From turn 11 onward, all damage increases by +10% per turn. By turn 15 (max), damage is +50%.

## Problem

Tank vs Tank matchups could stall indefinitely — both sides mitigating most damage, healing the rest. Fights hitting 15 turns with both players at 90%+ HP produced draws decided by rounding errors. Not fun.

## Solution

Escalating damage after turn 10 guarantees fights resolve decisively. The curve is gentle enough that aggressive builds aren't affected (fights end by turn 8–9 normally), but tanks can't hide behind armor forever.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Hard turn limit with sudden death:** Too binary, feels arbitrary
- **Healing reduction over time:** Only targets heal-heavy builds, doesn't solve armor stacking
- **Flat damage bonus:** Doesn't create escalating tension
