The loop

Choose stance + talents → STRIKE → picker slides out, Round Result card slides in. Bottom CTA row stays on screen — STRIKE button morphs into a passive "YOUR CHOICE · ATK [zone] / DEF [zone]" badge (gold border, dark fill). Skip dims out. After result → card slides out, picker back, STRIKE re-enables, round counter +1.

Log format (Summoner's-style)

Each exchange is a mini combat log — one line per event, text on one side, asset icon on the other. Ally rows flow left→right, enemy rows flow right→left. Lines stagger in with 120ms delay each.

Event types:
• Hit with [ability] on [zone] — (damage) type · armor reduction
• CRIT — gold glow on damage number
• Blocked / Dodged — muted italic, shield/dodge icon
• Talent fires — gold-bordered icon, additional damage + status applied
• Applied [status] for N turns — effect icon

Divider "Counter" separates your line(s) from enemy's. Sides alternate visual direction — makes exchange feel like dialogue, not bullet list.

Fighter cards stay

Portraits, HP bars, and chip stacks remain visible during the result. HP numbers animate down with red flash; damage numbers pop over portraits. This gives continuity — player sees HP change and the "why" at once.

Timing

Result card auto-dismisses after 1.8s. Tap anywhere on the card → skip to next round immediately. Talent fire extends by 0.6s.
