Hexbound is a dark fantasy RPG built around one thing: the duel.

Pick a Warrior, Rogue, Mage or Tank. Choose an origin — Human, Orc, Skeleton, Demon or Dogfolk — and forge a hero that is yours. Then step into the Arena.

TACTICAL PVP
Every fight is a battle of reads. Choose where you strike and where you guard, round by round. Predict your opponent and your blow lands clean; guess wrong and you eat the counter. No auto-battle, no pay-to-win stats — just you, your build and your nerve.

RANKED LADDER
Climb from Bronze to Grandmaster. Revenge matches let you settle the score with anyone who beat you. Seasons reset the board and reward the climb.

DUNGEONS & BOSSES
Eight hand-crafted dungeons, each with ten bosses and their own mechanics. Clear floors for gear, shards and gold. Dungeon Rush throws endless waves at you for the leaderboard.

BUILD YOUR HERO
Allocate stats, unlock a passive talent tree, equip active skills, and upgrade gear from common to legendary. Prestige at max level for a permanent edge.

TOWN LIFE
Run the Gold Mine while you are away, try your luck at the Fortune Wheel, trade with the Scavenger, take on daily quests and a seasonal Battle Pass.

GUILD HALL
Add allies, send challenges, exchange scrolls. Message any player, report anyone who steps out of line.

Hexbound is free to play. Optional purchases (gems, bundles, the Premium Pass subscription) speed things up but never change the odds of a duel.
