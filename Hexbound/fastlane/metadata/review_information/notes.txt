Hexbound is an online RPG and needs an internet connection.

SIGN-IN
No account is needed: tap PLAY on the welcome screen to play as a guest. Email, Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in are also offered. Demo account: see Sign-In Information above.

WHERE THINGS ARE (starting from the Hub)
- PvP: Arena > pick an opponent. Opponents are other players' characters (their gear, stats and runes) played by the AI, so PvP is asynchronous.
- Combat: each side rolls an attack die and a guard die every round; Focus pays for a reroll (2) or for setting a face (4).
- Dungeons: the ADVENTURES button at the bottom of the Hub.
- Purchases: Shop > GET MORE. Bundles tab: Starter Bundle and Adventurer's Bundles I-III. Gems tab: five gem packs. Special tab: Monthly Gem Card and Premium Pass. RESTORE PURCHASES is on this screen and on the Premium Pass screen.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Every one-off purchase is a consumable. The Starter Bundle can be bought once per account: the server enforces it and the offer disappears after purchase. The Monthly Gem Card gives 150 gems at once and 50 gems a day for 30 days (credited when the game is opened each day); buying it again while it is active adds 30 days. Premium Pass is a monthly auto-renewable subscription; its screen shows the price, the period, the renewal terms and links to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

SIMULATED GAMBLING
The Tavern's Fortune Wheel and Shell Game are played with in-game gold only. Nothing can be cashed out and there are no real-money prizes. Gold is earned in play; some bundles also include gold. The Fortune Wheel shows its odds. The Shell Game is a fair 1-in-3 pick (the server picks the winning shell before the player chooses) and a win pays twice the wager. The app is rated 18+.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Character names and messages are filtered on the server. Report a player from any player's profile (Report player); report a message in Guild Hall > Scrolls by long-pressing it (Report message). Allies can be blocked from the Allies tab. Reports are reviewed in our admin console.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Account > Delete Account.
