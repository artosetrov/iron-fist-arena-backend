# Hexbound — Currency Gem Prompts (Gem Tiers)

> **Status:** active, indexed
> **Style:** Main game style (pen and ink) — see `ART_STYLE_GUIDE.md`
> **Format:** 1:1 square, 4K, transparent or white background
> **Usage:** `CurrencyPurchaseView.swift` — 5 gem purchase tiers

---

## General Rules for All Gem Assets

- Style: Pen and ink illustration, bold black ink outlines on every element, colored ink with visible brushstrokes
- Shading: Crosshatching on metal/stone; ink wash for crystal luminance
- Composition: Isolated object on white/transparent background, no scenery, no characters, no text
- Color palette base: Dark iron gray, aged stone, charcoal, dark leather
- Color accent: Deep violet/purple (#8B5CF6, #A855F7) and cyan-white (#E0E7FF) highlights on facets
- Each tier = fundamentally different container/arrangement, not just "more gems"
- Every surface looks **aged and worn** — corroded metal, cracked stone, frayed cloth
- Recurring motifs: hexagonal facets, arcane runes, small skulls, chains
- Crystals have visible internal fracture lines, imperfections — nothing pristine
- NO glow effects, NO fog, NO atmospheric lighting, NO blur, NO gradient backgrounds
- Negative prompt for all: `painting, oil painting, watercolor, digital painting, concept art, realistic, photorealistic, 3D render, soft edges, blurry, atmospheric lighting, volumetric light, fog, mist, glow effects, gradient background, smooth shading, airbrushed, anime, pixel art`
