# Hexbound — Fortune Wheel Asset Prompts

> **Status**: `canonical` — Active prompt collection for Fortune Wheel minigame assets.
> **Style reference**: `docs/08_prompts/ART_STYLE_GUIDE.md`

## Назначение

Полный сет ассетов для экрана **Fortune Wheel** (мини-игра в таверне).
Стиль: pen and ink illustration с акварельной заливкой — идентичен остальным Hexbound-ассетам.

---

## 1. `fortune-wheel-bg` — Background (9:16)

Фон экрана колеса фортуны. Вертикальный мобильный формат.

```
Pen and ink illustration with watercolor wash coloring of a grimdark medieval fantasy fortune teller's alcove, vertical composition 9:16 for mobile game background. Heavy bold black ink outlines on every element. Watercolor washes in muted earth tones.

SETTING: A dark circular stone chamber inside a medieval tavern — an alcove dedicated to games of fate. Gothic stone archway entrance at the top. Rough stone walls with carved rune symbols and mystical glyphs etched into the rock — rendered as thin ink lines. Ceiling is vaulted dark stone with cobwebs drawn as thin ink threads.

CENTER (critical — must be dark and open): The middle 60% of the image must be DARK and relatively EMPTY — this is where the game UI wheel will overlay. A faint circular marking on the stone floor (chalk circle with mystical symbols) hints at where the wheel sits, but keep it very subtle and dark. Deep shadows dominate the center.

EDGES AND BORDERS: Left side — tall iron candelabra with dripping black candles, warm golden-orange watercolor glow around flames. Scattered animal skulls and bone fragments on a stone shelf. Right side — a heavy wooden cabinet with mysterious bottles and vials, some containing glowing purple-blue liquid (muted jewel-tone watercolor wash). Chains hanging from the ceiling. A crystal ball on a small ornate stand, catching faint purple light.

TOP AREA: The gothic stone archway with carved skull keystone at the apex. Tattered dark purple curtains hanging on either side — fabric folds rendered with ink crosshatching and dark violet-purple watercolor wash. Two wall-mounted torches flanking the arch with warm orange glow.

BOTTOM AREA: Edge of a wooden table visible at the bottom — scattered gold coins, a worn leather pouch, dice, and a tattered deck of fate cards. An old parchment map partially unrolled. A human skull used as a candle holder with melted wax dripping down.

ATMOSPHERE: Very dark and mystical. Warm golden-orange light from candles and torches creates small pools of light. Deep purple-blue magical undertones in the shadows. The feeling is: a sacred/cursed space where fate itself is gambled. NOT bright, NOT colorful — ominous, mysterious, ancient.

COLOR PALETTE: Very dark base (near-black #08080C to dark blue-gray #1A1A2E). Warm golden-orange (#D4A537) for candlelight. Deep purple (#9B59B6) for mystical accents. Dark stone gray with crosshatching. NO glow effects, NO blur, NO fog. Pen and ink only.

STYLE: Pen and ink illustration with watercolor wash. Bold black ink outlines visible on everything. NOT digital painting, NOT concept art, NOT pixel art. Grimdark dark fantasy. No text, no UI elements.
```

## 2. `fortune-wheel-texture` — Wheel Face Texture (1:1, 1024×1024)

Текстура самого колеса (вид сверху, как будто смотришь прямо на колесо).

```
Pen and ink illustration with watercolor wash of a medieval fortune wheel viewed directly from above, perfectly circular, isolated on pure white background. 1:1 square composition, 1024x1024 pixels. Heavy bold black ink outlines.

THE WHEEL: A circular wooden wheel divided into exactly 12 equal pie-slice sectors by bold dark iron spoke lines radiating from center to rim. The wheel is made of aged dark wood — oak-brown watercolor wash with wood grain texture drawn as parallel ink lines.

SECTORS (12 total, alternating):
- 6 DARK SECTORS (lose): Very dark wood, almost black — minimal decoration, a small skull or crossbones scratched into the wood with thin ink lines. Dark charcoal-brown watercolor wash.
- 3 GOLD SECTORS (x1.5 win): Warm golden-brown wood — gold-ochre watercolor wash. A single gold coin symbol etched into the wood. Thin gold leaf accent lines along sector edges.
- 1 BRIGHT GOLD SECTOR (x2 win): Brighter golden wood with more gold leaf decoration. Two stacked gold coins etched. Slightly more ornate than x1.5 sectors.
- 1 PURPLE SECTOR (x3 win): Dark wood with purple-tinted varnish — deep violet watercolor wash over brown base. A crown symbol etched into the wood with purple ink accent.
- 1 CYAN/BLUE SECTOR (x5 jackpot): Dark wood with teal-blue mystical runes — cyan watercolor accents. A star or pentagram symbol etched in. Most ornate sector with additional rune markings along the edges.

RIM: Heavy iron rim band around the outside — dark gray with rust spots (rust-orange watercolor). Decorative iron studs/rivets evenly spaced (12 total, one per sector boundary). The rim shows age — scratches, dents, corrosion.

CENTER: A large iron hub/boss at the center with a skull motif or compass rose design — dark iron with gold accents. An iron pin/axle in the very center.

SPOKE DIVIDERS: Thin iron strips separating each sector — dark gray with slight gold edge highlights where they meet the rim.

STYLE: Pen and ink illustration with watercolor wash. Viewed from DIRECTLY ABOVE (top-down). Bold black ink outlines. Wood grain texture visible. Aged, worn, ancient — NOT clean, NOT new. Grimdark dark fantasy. Isolated on white background, no shadows underneath. No text, no numbers.
```

## 3. `lady-fortuna-portrait` — Lady Fortuna NPC Portrait (1:1, 512×512)

Портрет NPC Lady Fortuna — хозяйка колеса в таверне.

```
Pen and ink illustration with watercolor wash of Lady Fortuna, a mysterious fortune teller character for a grimdark dark fantasy mobile game. Square portrait composition 1:1. Isolated on aged parchment-colored background. Heavy bold black ink outlines.

CHARACTER: A tall, elegant but sinister woman. NOT beautiful in a conventional way — striking, unsettling, otherworldly. Gaunt angular face with high cheekbones, slightly too-large dark eyes with a faint purple glow in the irises. Long nose, thin lips with a knowing smirk. Pale grayish skin — very light watercolor wash with blue-gray undertones. Long flowing dark hair, almost black with streaks of white or silver — ink lines for individual hair strands.

ATTIRE: A hooded dark purple-black cloak over layered robes — rich dark purple-violet watercolor wash for the cloak, deep burgundy for inner robes. The hood is pushed back, revealing her face. Heavy gold jewelry — a large circular medallion with a wheel of fate symbol on her chest (gold watercolor wash with ink detail). Multiple gold rings on long bony fingers. A chain belt with small vials and a key hanging from it.

ACCESSORIES: She holds a large ornate tarot card in one hand — the Wheel of Fortune card visible with a wheel symbol on it (gold ink detail on dark card). Her other hand has long fingers with golden fingertips (painted nails or gold thimbles). A small crystal sphere hangs from a chain at her belt.

HEAD: A dark purple headwrap or turban with a golden brooch at the front — the brooch has a wheel of fate design. A single long earring with a dangling skull charm on her left ear.

EXPRESSION: A knowing, slightly sinister smile — she knows the outcome before you spin. Eyes looking directly at the viewer. Confident, mysterious, slightly mocking.

POSE: Upper body portrait, 3/4 view facing slightly left. One hand raised showing the tarot card. Dramatic, like she's about to reveal your fate.

STYLE: Pen and ink illustration with watercolor wash on aged parchment background. Bold black ink outlines, visible crosshatching for shadows. Watercolor washes for color — dark purple, burgundy, gold, gray skin. Grotesque/exaggerated proportions — longer fingers, sharper features, larger eyes than natural. Like a D&D Monster Manual NPC portrait. No text, no UI elements.
```

## Генерация

**Рекомендуемые генераторы**: Midjourney v6+, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion XL с ControlNet

**Порядок генерации**:
1. `lady-fortuna-portrait` — самый сложный, начни с него
2. `fortune-wheel-bg` — фон экрана
3. `fortune-wheel-texture` — текстура колеса
4. `fortune-sector-icons` (5 шт.) — иконки секторов
5. `fortune-pointer` — указатель
6. `fortune-spin-button` — декор кнопки

**Формат выхода**: PNG с прозрачным фоном (кроме bg и texture).

**Post-processing**:
- Убрать белый фон → прозрачный (для иконок, указателя, кнопки)
- Подогнать яркость/контраст под палитру Hexbound
- Иконки секторов сделать @2x и @3x для iOS Asset Catalog
