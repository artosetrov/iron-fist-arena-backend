Issues with Current Flow

  • 5 equal CTAs on Welcome — paradox of choice. Eye doesn't know where to start.
  • Apple/Google repeated 3 times across screens. Redundant and dilutes the value of reaching Login/Signup.
  • Back arrow on Welcome — where does it go? Welcome is the root.
  • "Connect with" on Login is semantically wrong for authentication.
  • Social buttons AFTER form on Create Account — confusing placement.
  • PLAY AS GUEST is #1 CTA + dim warning below = contradiction. We push to the path we warn against.

One screen, two modes. No social buttons here — already offered on Welcome.

Option A — Guest-first

  • 1 primary CTA — Play as Guest. Zero-friction entry, perfect for curious users.
  • Social auth once, right below, as the "serious" path.
  • Email auth as text link — available but doesn't compete visually with Play and social.
  • Unified Sign in / Sign up screen with tab toggle — user can switch without going back.
  • 7 CTAs total across 2 screens (vs 14 across 3).
  • Trade-off: optimizes for first-session retention. Lower account creation rate day-1.

Before / After

  • Current: 3 screens · 14 CTAs · Apple/Google × 3 · "Play as Guest" primary with warning below
  • Recommended: 2 screens · 7 CTAs · Apple/Google × 1 · "PLAY AS GUEST" = the hero of the first screen
  • Saved: 1 screen, 7 CTAs, 2 duplicate social-button pairs, 1 stray back arrow
  • Kept: all three auth paths (guest / social / email) — just with a clear hierarchy
