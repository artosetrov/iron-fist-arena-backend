What to compare

  • A Replace. Modal card swaps in where the picker was. Clean, one source. Log rows are gone — you see verdict + clash + deltas and nothing else.
  • B Augment. The existing gold-bordered log card gets a verdict header + clash chip row. Detailed strike/crit/blocked rows stay below. Safer for v1.
  • C Hybrid. Middle outcomes use the compact B-style inline card. Peaks (OUTPLAYED / OUTREAD) break out to a dimmed full-stage overlay. Spectacle reserved for emotional beats.

Things to feel for

  • Does the OUTPLAYED gold pulse hit hard enough in A and B, or does it need the C treatment?
  • In B, is the card too tall when log rows + clash + verdict all stack? Check OUTPLAYED + Crit.
  • In C, does the peak overlay feel too heavy to fire twice in 6 rounds? Or does the scarcity make it land?
  • Auto-dismiss is 1.4s on middle outcomes, 2.0s on peaks in C. Watch the drain bar at the bottom of each card.
