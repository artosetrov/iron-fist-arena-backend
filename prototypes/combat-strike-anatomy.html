What changes vs v3.1. Matrix flips from 2 stacked rows (ATTACK row + DEFEND row) to 2 vertical columns flanking a body silhouette. Head-tile sits next to the head, legs-tile sits next to the feet — players don't have to read "HEAD / CHEST / LEGS" labels because the spatial anatomy already tells them. Selecting a tile lights up the matching body region (red glow for ATK, blue glow for DEF). Active Skills section grows from 3 → 4 slots (3 talent slots + 1 consumable slot with stack count).
