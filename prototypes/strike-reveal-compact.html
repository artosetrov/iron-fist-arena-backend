Compact vs Full Screen — Trade-offs

  • Compact wins: keeps combat screen spatial context (HP bars, portraits, zones all still visible behind dim), less per-turn friction, easier to iterate on reveal timing.
  • Full screen wins: more emotional punch on the OUTPLAYED / OUTREAD quadrants, room for bigger clash breakdowns, hides HP drain animation surprise.
  • Hybrid option: compact modal by default, full-screen reveal only on double-win and double-miss (the emotional peaks).

Tap vs Auto-dismiss

  • Auto (~1.4s): faster match pacing, feels toast-like. Risk: Outread goes by too quick to internalize the miss.
  • Tap: one extra tap per turn, but lets player feel the outcome before moving on. Better for RPS read-game.
  • Suggestion: Tap on OUTPLAYED / OUTREAD (peaks), Auto on Held / Struck / Traded (middle ground). Player-driven where it matters.
