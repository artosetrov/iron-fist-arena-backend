  • Offense read ✓ = your atk zone ≠ their def zone → you hit a weak spot.
  • Defense read ✓ = their atk zone = your def zone → you blocked the incoming hit.
  • CRIT / DODGE layer on top as gold flourishes (✦ crit on a STRUCK, 👁 dodge on a HELD).

Reveal choreography (~800ms)

  • 0ms — STRIKE pressed. Button locks.
  • 0–300ms — enemy chips fade in (opacity only).
  • 200ms — verdict banner fades in with color + glow. For OUTPLAYED, a 22% gold screen flash pulses once.
  • 400ms — clash cards fade in. Winning axis card gets a gold border + soft glow; losing axis card is at 72% opacity.
  • 500ms — HP bars drain. On OUTPLAYED, enemy portrait dims to loser state.

Why this communicates the RPS stakes

  • Banner sets the emotion before the player reads the numbers. You feel the outcome, then verify it.
  • Card emphasis tells you which axis you won or lost. One glance locates the skill moment.
  • Gold reserved for the double-win keeps it special — not every turn gets celebrated the same way.
  • OUTREAD is painful but not punishing — red tint, dimmed cards, no flash. The feedback is clear without twisting the knife.

Try the four variants

  • Outplayed — CHEST atk hits exposed LEGS; HEAD def blocks their HEAD atk. Gold banner + flash.
  • Outplayed · CRIT + DODGE — the jackpot version. HEAD atk crits, LEGS def dodges. Banner adds a ✦ flourish.
  • Held the Line — offense read lost, defense read won. Green banner, incoming card glows gold.
  • Outread — both reads wrong. Red banner, both cards dimmed, red screen flash.
